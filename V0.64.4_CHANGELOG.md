# MemeX Market v0.64.4 — Memecoin Market Polish

## Launch-state correctness
- Creator launch bootstrap is now stored as `is_launch_seed=true` and is not treated as a public market trade.
- New coins start public market history at 0% change, 0 public volume and 0 public trades until another market participant trades.
- The launch candle is flat at the actual post-bootstrap market opening price and has zero public volume.
- Existing v200 coins are repaired conservatively by matching only the creator's Genesis #1 trade against recorded launch amounts.
- Public market overview, coin detail history, portfolio history, feed, achievements, creator analytics and profile financial stats exclude launch seed rows.

## Compact memecoin terminal
- Removed the large always-visible metrics wall from the main coin screen.
- Metrics now live in a compact `Метрики` sheet.
- Mobile coin detail is a one-screen terminal: identity, chart, tabs and buy/sell fit inside the Telegram viewport without page-level scrolling on normal phone heights.
- Orders, holders and trades are switched by compact tabs instead of stacking vertically.
- Trade controls are denser and keep balance, amount, quick percentages and slippage close together.
- Tiny prices use compact scientific formatting on the chart axis instead of long strings of 9s/0s.
- A pristine coin shows a neutral chart state until the first real public trade.

## Creator launch preview
- Creation UI calls the bootstrap a `Стартовый резерв`, not a public first trade.
- Preview shows the calculated post-seed opening price and AMM liquidity before creation.

Apply `supabase/migrations/100002_memecoin_market_polish_v0644.sql` before deploying the v0.64.4 frontend. The migration bumps the required schema version to 201.
